Engineered to Outperform Under Harsh Grid Dynamics

Every transformer manufactured at our Chatra facility adheres to stringent thermodynamic, dielectric, and mechanical standards. Here is why India's leading power authorities trust RV Electricals.

Prime Laser-Scribed CRGO Core

Built with imported M3, M4, and Hi-B grade Cold Rolled Grain Oriented silicon steel. Mitered 45-degree step-lap joints minimize no-load losses and eliminate harmonic humming.

  • Lowest watt-loss per kilogram guaranteed
  • Full step-lap automated stacking
  • Rigid tie-rod core clamping against grid shock

99.9% Electrolytic Copper Windings

Oxygen-free high-conductivity electrolytic copper conductors wrapped with thermally upgraded insulation paper. Designed with specialized cooling ducts for uniform oil circulation.

  • Continuous disc & helical winding structures
  • High short-circuit withstand dynamic strength
  • Low hot-spot temperature rise margin

Deep Vacuum Drying & Oil Impregnation

High-vacuum heating chambers extract residual moisture below 0.5%. Filled under continuous vacuum with filtered transformer oil possessing breakdown voltage > 65 kV.

  • De-aerated, moisture-free insulation matrix
  • Shot-blasted, epoxy powder-coated leakproof tank
  • Hermetically sealed with premium elastomeric gaskets

100% In-House Electrical Testing Lab

Every unit undergoes rigorous routine and dielectric testing aligned with NABL and CPRI protocols: lightning impulse, temperature rise, turn ratio, and loss verification.

  • Calibrated computerized test bench
  • Zero-defect dispatch inspection protocol
  • Complete test certificates provided with each batch

BEE Star Rated & Eco-Conscious

Engineered to meet the Bureau of Energy Efficiency (BEE) Star-1 to Star-3 guidelines (IS 1180 Part-1), dramatically cutting operational energy bills and carbon emissions.

  • Options for biodegradable ester insulating fluid
  • Ultra-low acoustic decibel performance
  • Designed for 25+ years trouble-free field life

Rapid Field Response & Turnkey AMC

Our dedicated engineering support cell provides rapid on-site breakdown assistance, oil centrifuging, bushing replacements, and annual maintenance throughout Eastern India.

  • 24/7 emergency utility breakdown helpline
  • Genuine factory spares readily stocked
  • Mobile oil filtration & BDV testing units
