Lifecycle Engineering

Comprehensive Substation & Transformer Services

Maximizing asset longevity, eliminating unscheduled downtime, and guaranteeing grid performance with 24/7 mobile engineering teams.

High-Vacuum Oil Filtration & Dehydration

Our mobile vacuum oil processing units restore degraded transformer oil on-site without de-energizing or de-tanking when permissible.

  • Dielectric breakdown voltage (BDV) restored above 65 kV
  • Moisture content reduced below 10 ppm
  • Particulate matter filtration down to 0.5 micron

Complete Overhauling & Core Re-winding

Full factory overhauling of burnt or failed transformers up to 50 MVA. Includes re-winding with 99.9% electrolytic copper and high-vacuum dehydration.

  • Complete core cleaning & re-varnishing
  • Replacement of deteriorated gaskets & bushings
  • Post-repair full routine testing & warranty

Advanced Electrical Diagnostic & DGA Lab

In-depth analytical testing to diagnose latent faults, overheating hot spots, and insulation degradation before catastrophic grid failure occurs.

  • Dissolved Gas Analysis (DGA) for thermal/electrical arcing
  • Insulation Resistance (IR) & Polarization Index (PI)
  • Tan Delta & Capacitance testing for bushings and oil

Annual Maintenance Contracts (AMC)

Customized maintenance packages for industrial substations, utilities, and solar parks with strict response time service level agreements (SLAs).

  • Quarterly infrared thermography scans
  • Breather silica gel & Buchholz relay calibration
  • Priority emergency breakdown mobilization
