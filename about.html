About RV Electricals Industries

Powering Progress Through Engineering Integrity

From our manufacturing plant in Chatra, Jharkhand to regional electricity boards across India, RV Electricals Industries delivers transformers built to conquer severe electrical grid demands.

Our Heritage & Mission

A Decade of Dedicated Power Innovation

Established with the conviction that domestic heavy engineering can rival the world’s finest, RV Electricals Industries has developed into one of Eastern India’s foremost manufacturers of electrical transformers and allied power apparatus.

Headquartered with an integrated production, assembly, and testing facility in Vill.-Bela, Mayurhand (Chatra, Jharkhand), we serve State Electricity Boards (such as JBVNL), central public sector power majors like NTPC and DVC, as well as renewable energy solar parks, steel rolling mills, and commercial infrastructure developers.

Our Mission

To engineer high-efficiency, zero-defect power transformers that empower utilities, drive industrial growth, and slash transmission losses.

Our Vision

To be the benchmark for electrical transformer reliability, recognized for technological innovation and unmatched customer stewardship.

Manufacturing Infrastructure

Built for Heavy-Duty Capacity & Precision

Our dedicated factory floor integrates computerized core slitting, cleanroom coil winding, high-vacuum dehydration, and full routine testing.

25,000+ Sq. Ft. Covered Bays

Spacious, dust-controlled fabrication and core-stacking bays equipped with 20-tonne overhead gantry cranes for safely maneuvering heavy transformer tanks and sub-assemblies.

Vacuum Drying & Impregnation Plant

Automated industrial vacuum heating ovens capable of extracting moisture below 0.5% from insulation paper, coupled with high-flow oil centrifuging units achieving BDV > 65 kV.

NABL-Aligned High Voltage Test Bay

Fully screened testing cell with computerized loss measurement consoles, separate-source AC high voltage sets, double-voltage double-frequency (DVDF) motor generators, and megger stations.
